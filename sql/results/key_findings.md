# Key Findings

## Sales

- Total orders: 99,441
- Delivered orders: 96,477
- Delivered-order revenue: 15,422,461.77
- Average order value: 159.86
- Revenue increased significantly from 2017 to 2018.
- November 2017 recorded the highest monthly revenue in the available period.

## Customers

- 93,099 customers made one purchase.
- 2,997 customers were repeat customers.
- Repeat customers spent approximately 314.99 on average.
- One-time customers spent approximately 161.82 on average.
- Customer retention is therefore an important growth opportunity.

## Products

Top categories by allocated revenue:

1. beleza_saude — 258,385.79
2. relogios_presentes — 217,319.41
3. cama_mesa_banho — 214,332.92
4. esporte_lazer — 202,319.88
5. informatica_acessorios — 192,796.58

## Geography

- São Paulo had the highest order volume with 5,180 orders.
- São Paulo also generated the highest revenue.
- Average order value varied considerably across states.
- Delivery times also varied substantially by state.

## Delivery

- Average delivery time: 12.50 days.
- Late deliveries: 7,826.
- Late delivery rate: 8.11%.
- São Paulo had an average delivery time of approximately 8.73 days.
- Some states recorded average delivery times above 20 days.

## Sellers

The seller analysis identified significant differences in:

- Revenue
- Order volume
- Items sold
- Delivery performance

The highest-revenue seller generated approximately 47,594.72 in product revenue.

## Customer Satisfaction

- On-time deliveries had an average review score of 4.24.
- Late deliveries had an average review score of 2.50.
- Late deliveries were associated with substantially lower customer ratings.

## Business Opportunities

1. Increase customer retention and repeat purchases.
2. Investigate causes of regional delivery delays.
3. Improve seller fulfillment performance.
4. Focus marketing on high-performing product categories.
5. Improve delivery reliability to strengthen customer satisfaction.
